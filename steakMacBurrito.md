# Steak & Mac Burritos

> **Source:** [ryanrubiotrains on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ryanrubiotrains/reel/DMRKxGWS_YQ/)

## Overview

**Yield:** 6 burritos  
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes

### Nutrition (per burrito)
- **Calories:** 475
- **Protein:** 55g
- **Carbs:** 52g
- **Fat:** 10g

---

## Ingredients

### For the Steak & Marinade
- 2 lbs top sirloin, cubed
- 125g (½ cup) orange juice
- 60g (¼ cup) soy sauce
- 1 lime, juiced
- 40g (2 tbsp) honey
- 8 cloves garlic
- Salt, pepper, cumin, and onion powder to taste

### For the Mac & Cheese
- 150g (5 oz) dry pasta
- 150g (⅔ cup) 1% cottage cheese
- 120g (1 cup) fat-free cheddar cheese
- 60g (¼ cup) milk

### For Assembly
- 6 large tortillas
- 1 handful fresh cilantro, chopped
- Sriracha to taste (optional)

---

## Directions

### 1. Prepare the Marinade
1. In a blender, combine orange juice, soy sauce, lime juice, honey, garlic, and seasonings (salt, pepper, cumin, onion powder)
2. Blend until smooth

### 2. Cook the Steak
1. Cube the top sirloin into bite-sized pieces
2. Marinate the steak (optional: marinate for 30 minutes to 2 hours for best flavor)
3. Cook steak to your desired doneness
4. Set aside

### 3. Make the Mac & Cheese
1. Boil pasta according to package directions until al dente
2. While pasta cooks, blend cottage cheese, cheddar cheese, and milk until smooth
3. Drain pasta and mix with the cheese sauce
4. Stir until well combined

### 4. Assemble the Burritos
1. Lay out tortillas on a clean surface
2. Add a portion of steak and mac & cheese to the center of each tortilla
3. Sprinkle with fresh cilantro and add Sriracha if desired
4. Fold in the sides and roll tightly into burritos

### 5. Finish & Store
1. Heat a pan over medium heat
2. Pan fry each burrito seam-side down until golden and crispy (2-3 minutes per side)
3. Let cool slightly before serving

---

## Storage

- **Refrigerator:** Store wrapped burritos for up to 4 days
- **Freezer:** Wrap individually in foil or plastic wrap and freeze for up to 3 months
- **Reheat:** Microwave for 2-3 minutes or bake at 350°F until heated through

---

## Tips & Variations

💡 **Pro Tips:**
- Marinate the steak longer for more flavor
- Use whole wheat tortillas for extra fiber
- Add black beans or peppers for more vegetables
- Double the batch and freeze for easy meal prep

🌶️ **Spice Level:** Adjust Sriracha to your heat preference
