Makes 6 Steak & Mac Burritos

475 cals | 55g protein | 52g carbs | 10g fat

COOKBOOK UPDATES EVERY SATURDAY. CHECK MY 🔗 TO GET YOUR COPY 

⸻

INGREDIENTS
	•	2lbs top sirloin
	•	125g orange juice
	•	60g soy sauce
	•	150g dry pasta
	•	1 lime
	•	40g honey
	•	1 handful cilantro
	•	8 cloves garlic
	•	150g 1% cottage cheese
	•	120g fat free cheddar
	•	60g milk
	•	Sriracha
	•	Salt, pepper, cumin, onion powder
	•	6 tortillas

⸻

DIRECTIONS
➡️ Blend OJ, soy, lime, honey, garlic, and seasonings for the marinade
➡️ Cube and cook steak to your liking, then set aside
➡️ Boil pasta — while that’s going, blend cottage cheese, cheddar, and milk
➡️ Mix the cheese sauce into cooked pasta
➡️ Lay out tortillas, add steak + mac, and roll into burritos
✅ Pan fry each burrito until crispy
✅ Store in fridge or freezer
