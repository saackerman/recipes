# High-Protein Steak Wagyu Meal Prep

These Steak Wagyu Bowls pack **50g of protein each** and are perfect for a week-long meal prep.

## Ingredients

- **Vegetables:** 4 bell peppers (various colors) `[00:03]`
- **Protein:** 3 lbs shredded beef steak `[00:21]`
- **Base:** 2 cups (400g) rice `[00:26]`
- **Cooking Liquid:** 9.5 oz bone broth `[00:28]`
- **Seasoning:** Cajun seasoning, paprika, black pepper `[00:15]`
- **Sauce:** 83g hot honey, 60g Sriracha `[00:35]`

## Instructions

### 1. Sauté the Peppers `[00:03]`

1. Dice up the four bell peppers.
2. Add them to a hot pan with Cajun seasoning, paprika, and black pepper `[00:15]`.
3. Cook for 5–10 minutes until softened `[00:18]`.

### 2. Cook the Steak `[00:21]`

1. Add 3 lbs of shredded beef steak to the pan with the peppers.
2. Mix well and cook for another 5–10 minutes `[00:24]`.

### 3. Prepare the Rice `[00:26]`

1. Add 2 cups of rice and 9.5 oz of bone broth to a rice cooker `[00:28]`.
2. Cook until the rice is fully done.

### 4. Combine and Sauce `[00:32]`

1. Add the cooked rice into the pan with the steak and peppers.
2. Stir in 83g of hot honey and 60g of Sriracha `[00:35]`.
3. Mix everything thoroughly to combine the flavors `[00:39]`.

### 5. Portion Out `[00:41]`

1. Divide the mixture evenly into eight meal prep containers.

## Nutritional Info

- **Protein:** 50g per serving `[00:00]`
- **Servings:** 8 portions
