High-Protein Steak Wagyu Meal Prep
These Steak Wagyu Bowls pack 50g of protein each and are perfect for a week-long meal prep.

Ingredients
Vegetables: 4 Bell Peppers (various colors) [00:03]

Protein: 3 lbs Shredded Beef Steak [00:21]

Base: 2 cups (400g) Rice [00:26]

Cooking Liquid: 9.5 oz Bone Broth [00:28]

Seasoning: Cajun Seasoning, Paprika, Black Pepper [00:15]

Sauce: 83g Hot Honey, 60g Sriracha [00:35]

Instructions
1. Sauté the Peppers [00:03]
Dice up the four bell peppers.

Add them to a hot pan with Cajun seasoning, paprika, and black pepper [00:15].

Cook for 5–10 minutes until softened [00:18].

2. Cook the Steak [00:21]
Add 3 lbs of shredded beef steak to the pan with the peppers.

Mix well and cook for another 5–10 minutes [00:24].

3. Prepare the Rice [00:26]
Add 2 cups of rice and 9.5 oz of bone broth to a rice cooker [00:28].

Cook until the rice is fully done.

4. Combine and Sauce [00:32]
Add the cooked rice into the pan with the steak and peppers.

Stir in 83g of hot honey and 60g of Sriracha [00:35].

Mix everything thoroughly to combine the flavors [00:39].

5. Portion Out [00:41]
Divide the mixture evenly into eight meal prep containers.

Nutritional Info
Protein: 50g per serving [00:00].

Servings: 8 portions.
