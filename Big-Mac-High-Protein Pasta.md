Big Mac Style High-Protein Pasta Meal Prep
This recipe provides a healthier, high-protein alternative to a Big Mac by combining lean beef, protein pasta, and a custom sugar-free sauce.

Ingredients
Protein & Base: 3 lbs Ground Beef [00:00], 1 box Protein Pasta [00:04]

Vegetables: Onions [00:20], Diced Tomatoes [00:24]

Seasoning: Garlic powder, Paprika [00:03]

Big Mac Sauce Ingredients: Plain Greek yogurt, sugar-free ketchup, yellow mustard, sweet relish, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, pepper, and white vinegar [00:09].

Instructions
1. Cook Beef & Pasta [00:00]
Add 3 lbs of ground beef to a pan and season with garlic powder and paprika.

In a separate pot, cook one box of protein pasta according to the package directions [00:04].

2. Prepare the Sauce [00:09]
In a bowl, mix together plain Greek yogurt, sugar-free ketchup, yellow mustard, sweet relish, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, pepper, and a splash of white vinegar.

3. Add Onions [00:20]
When the beef is halfway cooked, add in the diced onions and finish browning.

4. Combine Everything [00:23]
Once the beef and pasta are fully cooked, add the pasta into the pan with the beef.

Add the diced tomatoes [00:24].

Pour in the homemade Big Mac sauce [00:27].

5. Mix & Portion [00:29]
Thoroughly mix all ingredients until the pasta and beef are well-coated in the sauce.

Divide into meal prep containers for the week [00:32].
