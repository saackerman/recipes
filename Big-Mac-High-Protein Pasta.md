# Big Mac–Style High-Protein Pasta Meal Prep

A healthier, high-protein take on a Big Mac-inspired pasta: lean beef, protein pasta, and a homemade sugar-free "Big Mac" sauce. Perfect for meal prep and reheating throughout the week.

## Yield

4–6 servings (depending on container size)

## Total time

About 35–45 minutes

## Ingredients

### Protein & base
- 3 lb (1.36 kg) ground beef
- 1 box protein pasta (about 12–16 oz / 340–450 g)

### Vegetables
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 14–16 oz (400–450 g) diced tomatoes (canned or fresh)

### Seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### Big Mac–style sauce (makes ~1 cup)
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt (or light mayo for richer flavor)
- 1/3 cup sugar-free ketchup
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard
- 2 tbsp sweet relish
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp white vinegar (optional, for tang)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### Optional add-ins
- Pickles or extra relish
- Shredded lettuce for topping when serving

## Equipment
- Large skillet
- Pot for pasta
- Mixing bowl
- Meal-prep containers

## Instructions

1. Cook the beef
   - Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and season with garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
   - Break up the beef and cook until browned and fully cooked.

2. Cook the pasta
   - In a separate pot, cook the protein pasta according to package directions. Drain and set aside.

3. Prepare the Big Mac–style sauce
   - In a bowl, whisk together the plain Greek yogurt, sugar-free ketchup, yellow mustard, sweet relish, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, white vinegar, salt, and pepper. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.

4. Add onions and tomatoes
   - When the beef is nearly done, add the diced onion and cook until softened.
   - Stir in the diced tomatoes and simmer for 2–3 minutes to warm through and combine flavors.

5. Combine pasta, beef, and sauce
   - Add the drained pasta to the skillet with the beef and vegetables. Pour in the sauce and toss until everything is evenly coated and heated through.

6. Mix & portion
   - Taste and adjust seasoning. Divide the mixture evenly into meal-prep containers.
   - Optional: add shredded lettuce or pickle slices when serving for a more Big Mac–like texture.

## Storage
- Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave or a skillet until warmed through.
- Freeze for up to 2 months; thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

## Notes & substitutions
- Swap Greek yogurt for light mayo if you prefer a creamier, more traditional sauce.
- Use lean ground turkey or chicken for a lower-fat option.
- Adjust the sauce sweetness/saltiness by changing the amount/type of ketchup and relish.
