High Protein Mango Habanero Meal Prep Bowls
These instructions are based on the meal prep recipe by @Cookingforgains. This recipe yields 7 servings designed for fitness and weight loss goals.

Nutritional Information (Per Serving)
Calories: 471 kcal

Protein: 48g

Fats: 5.25g

Carbs: 62g

Ingredients
Stage 1: The Protein
Chicken: 3 lbs chopped chicken breast

Vegetables: 8 oz chopped onions, 8 oz chopped peppers

Spice Blend:

10g Paprika

10g Onion powder

8g Chili powder

6g Smoked paprika

5g Salt

4g Black pepper

3g Cumin

3g Crushed red pepper

2g Cayenne pepper

2g Ground mustard

2g Oregano

1g Cinnamon

Stage 2: The Carb Base
400g Uncooked Jasmine rice

3 cups Water

Stage 3: Mango "Habanero" Sauce
1 cup Plain Greek yogurt

300g Sliced mangoes (peeled)

21g Honey

7 Jalapeño peppers (adjust for heat)

1 tbsp Jalapeño pepper sauce

Instructions
1. Prepare the Rice
Weigh out 400g of jasmine rice.

Wash the rice thoroughly until the water runs clear.

Add the rice and 3 cups of water to a rice cooker and start the cooking cycle.

2. Cook the Chicken
Place the chopped chicken breast in a large pan or skillet.

Add the entire spice blend (paprika through cinnamon) to the chicken.

Cook over medium-high heat for approximately 5 minutes.

Add the chopped onions and chopped peppers to the pan.

Continue cooking until the chicken is fully cooked through and the vegetables are tender.

3. Blend the Sauce
In a blender, combine the Greek yogurt, mangoes, honey, jalapeños, and jalapeño sauce.

Blend for 10 seconds.

Stop and give the blender a good shake to ensure everything is settled.

Blend for another 10 seconds until smooth.

4. Assembly
In a large mixing bowl or in the pan (if large enough), combine the cooked chicken/vegetable mixture and the cooked jasmine rice.

Pour the blended mango sauce over the mixture.

Toss everything together until the rice and chicken are evenly coated in the sauce.

Divide the mixture equally into 7 meal prep containers.

Tips
Storage: These bowls can be kept in the refrigerator for up to 4-5 days.

Heat Level: Even though the recipe is titled "Mango Habanero," the ingredient list specifies jalapeños. If you prefer a more authentic "Habanero" heat, swap 1 or 2 jalapeños for a habanero pepper (be careful, they are much hotter!).
