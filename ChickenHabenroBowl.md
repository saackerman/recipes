# High Protein Mango Habanero Meal Prep Bowls

These instructions are based on the meal prep recipe by @Cookingforgains. This recipe yields **7 servings** designed for fitness and weight loss goals.

## Nutritional Information (Per Serving)

- **Calories:** 471 kcal
- **Protein:** 48g
- **Fats:** 5.25g
- **Carbs:** 62g

## Ingredients

### Stage 1: The Protein

- **Chicken:** 3 lbs chopped chicken breast
- **Vegetables:** 8 oz chopped onions, 8 oz chopped peppers

#### Spice Blend

- 10g paprika
- 10g onion powder
- 8g chili powder
- 6g smoked paprika
- 5g salt
- 4g black pepper
- 3g cumin
- 3g crushed red pepper
- 2g cayenne pepper
- 2g ground mustard
- 2g oregano
- 1g cinnamon

### Stage 2: The Carb Base

- 400g uncooked jasmine rice
- 3 cups water

### Stage 3: Mango "Habanero" Sauce

- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 300g sliced mangoes (peeled)
- 21g honey
- 7 jalapeño peppers (adjust for heat)
- 1 tbsp jalapeño pepper sauce

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Rice

1. Weigh out 400g of jasmine rice.
2. Wash the rice thoroughly until the water runs clear.
3. Add the rice and 3 cups of water to a rice cooker and start the cooking cycle.

### 2. Cook the Chicken

1. Place the chopped chicken breast in a large pan or skillet.
2. Add the entire spice blend (paprika through cinnamon) to the chicken.
3. Cook over medium-high heat for approximately 5 minutes.
4. Add the chopped onions and chopped peppers to the pan.
5. Continue cooking until the chicken is fully cooked through and the vegetables are tender.

### 3. Blend the Sauce

1. In a blender, combine the Greek yogurt, mangoes, honey, jalapeños, and jalapeño sauce.
2. Blend for 10 seconds.
3. Stop and give the blender a good shake to ensure everything is settled.
4. Blend for another 10 seconds until smooth.

### 4. Assembly

1. In a large mixing bowl or in the pan (if large enough), combine the cooked chicken/vegetable mixture and the cooked jasmine rice.
2. Pour the blended mango sauce over the mixture.
3. Toss everything together until the rice and chicken are evenly coated in the sauce.
4. Divide the mixture equally into 7 meal prep containers.

## Tips

- **Storage:** These bowls can be kept in the refrigerator for up to 4-5 days.
- **Heat Level:** Even though the recipe is titled "Mango Habanero," the ingredient list specifies jalapeños. If you prefer a more authentic "Habanero" heat, swap 1 or 2 jalapeños for a habanero pepper.
