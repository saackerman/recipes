Here is the recipe for the **High-Protein Taco Stuffed Sweet Potato Bowl** from the [video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU):

### **Ingredients**

* Sweet potatoes  
* Lean ground beef  
* Onion, finely chopped  
* Minced garlic  
* Tomato paste  
* Bone broth  
* Fresh lime juice  
* Fresh parsley  
* **Seasoning Blend:** *Referenced in video as a custom mix*  
* **Toppings:** *Optional green toppings/garnishes*

### **Instructions**

#### **1\. Prep and Cook the Potatoes**

* Poke holes all over the sweet potatoes using a fork to prevent them from puffing up or bursting while baking \[[00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=13)\].  
* Bake them in the oven until tender \[[00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=13)\].

#### **2\. Cook the Taco Beef Fill**

* Finely chop the onions and add them to a pan over medium-high heat. Cook until they are golden brown \[[00:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=17)\].  
* Stir in the minced garlic and cook until highly fragrant \[[00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=21)\].  
* Add the lean ground beef to the pan and cook until browned \[[00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=25)\].  
* Season the beef mixture, then mix in a bit of tomato paste and bone broth for added flavor depth \[[00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=25)\].  
* Finish the filling by tossing in fresh lime juice and chopped parsley \[[00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=34)\].

#### **3\. Assemble the Bowls**

* Slice the cooked sweet potatoes open and stuff them generously with the taco beef mixture \[[00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=38)\].  
* Garnish with extra green toppings to finish \[[00:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgr82fnv2sU&t=42)\].
