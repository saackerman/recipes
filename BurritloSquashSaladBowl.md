# High-Protein, High-Volume Dinner Bowl Recipe

This recipe is a staple "99% of the time" dinner designed for high volume, high protein (~60g), and low calories. It creates a satisfying, large bowl of food often described as "drunk 3:00 a.m. Chipotle vibes."

## Ingredients & Preparation

### 1. The Base: Greens [00:00:06]
* Start with a generous bed of your favorite mixed greens or salad base.

### 2. The Creamy Element [00:00:13]
* **Cottage Cheese** or **Low-fat Greek Yogurt**: Add a substantial scoop on top of the greens. Both options provide high protein and a creamy texture.

### 3. The Volume: Baked Squash [00:00:19]
* **Kabocha Squash** (or any preferred squash variety): Bake the squash and season with salt. Use a significant amount to "top up" the plate for maximum volume.

### 4. The Protein: Extra Lean Ground Beef [00:00:37]
* Add a portion of cooked extra lean ground beef to reach the high protein target (approx. 60g total).

### 5. Flavor & Toppings [00:00:37]
* **Fresh Salsa**: "Absolutely dump it" over the bowl for moisture and flavor.
* **Hot Sauce**: Add your favorite hot sauce (e.g., Valentina) [00:00:44].
* **Optional**: Add avocado for extra healthy fats if desired [00:00:44].

### 6. The "Mush Effect" [00:00:50]
* Mix everything together thoroughly to create a cohesive, "food-style bowl."

---

## Nutritional Highlights
* **Protein**: Approximately 60 grams [00:01:26].
* **Volume**: A very large serving size ("the size of your head") for relatively low calories.
