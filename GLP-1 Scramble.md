Here is the recipe for Madelaine Rascan's **"GLP-1 Scramble,"** [[00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=9)] a high-protein breakfast designed to keep you full for hours. It sounds like an unusual combination of savory, sweet, and tangy, but as she puts it, it's her absolute favorite [[00:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=43)].

### Ingredients

**For the Scramble:**

* **Eggs & Whites:** 1 whole egg + 2 egg whites (to beef up the protein) [[00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=12)]
* **Squash:** Pre-baked squash cubes (she loves Kabocha, but Butternut or Acorn work great too) [[00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=27)]
* **Oil:** Avocado oil spray [[00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=22)]
* **Seasoning:** A good pinch of salt [[01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=63)]

**For the Bowl Base:**

* **Yogurt:** 2 big scoops of non-fat Greek yogurt [[00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=37)]
* **Herbs:** A generous amount of dried dill (or chives if you have them) [[00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=48)]
* **Seasoning:** A solid crack of salt [[00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=54)]

**Toppings:**

* **Everything Bagel Seasoning:** (Referred to as "AO" in the video) [[01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=73)]
* **Sauce:** A teaspoon of Coconut Aminos (or a splash of Tamari sauce as a backup) [[01:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=81)]

---

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Prep the Yogurt Base:** 1-2 mins.
In your serving bowl, mix together the non-fat Greek yogurt, dried dill, and a crack of salt until smooth. Spread it across the bottom to create a base layer.


2. **Sauté the Squash:** 2-3 mins.
Lightly spray a pan with avocado oil and heat it up. Toss in your pre-baked squash cubes to get them warmed through.


3. **Whisk and Scramble:** 2 mins.
Whisk the 1 whole egg and 2 egg whites together in a small bowl with a pinch of salt. Pour the egg mixture directly over the warm squash in the pan and scramble everything together until cooked.


4. **Assemble and Top:** 1 min.
Slide the hot egg and squash scramble right on top of your seasoned yogurt base. Top with everything bagel seasoning and drizzle about a teaspoon of coconut aminos over the whole thing.


---

> **Substitution Tip:** If you don't have squash, you can use sweet potato instead, though the calories will be slightly higher [[01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=67)].

When eating, make sure to get a little bit of everything on your fork—the squash, egg, cool yogurt, and savory toppings together make the magic happen! [[01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPgoHcWtO0c&t=91)]
