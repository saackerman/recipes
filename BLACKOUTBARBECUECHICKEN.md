# BLACKOUT BARBECUE CHICKEN

**Yield:** 10 servings  
**Macros (approx., per serving):** 800–830 calories • 63–66g protein • 63–67g carbs • 36–38g fat

## Ingredients

### Protein
- 4 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs  
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast  

### Sauce
- 1½ cups smoky barbecue sauce  
- 6 oz tomato paste  
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard  
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce  
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar  
- 1 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp brown sugar  
- 1 tbsp hot sauce  
- ½ cup chicken broth  

### Seasoning Blend
- 2 tbsp smoked paprika  
- 1½ tbsp chili powder  
- 1 tbsp garlic powder  
- 1 tbsp onion powder  
- 2 tsp ground mustard  
- 2 tsp black pepper  
- 1½ tsp sea salt  
- 1 tsp cayenne  
- ½ tsp cinnamon  

### Finish
- 8 oz cream cheese  
- 8 oz shredded sharp cheddar  

### Rice
- 3 cups dry jasmine rice  
- 3 cups water  

## Instructions
1. Cook on **HIGH for 3 hours**.
2. Shred the chicken.
3. Let the sauce **reduce for 10 minutes**.
4. Stir in the **cream cheese**.
5. Add the **cheddar** and stir until melted.
6. Portion evenly into **10 servings**.
