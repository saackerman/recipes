BLACKOUT BARBECUE CHICKEN
10 Servings
Approx. 800–830 Calories
63–66g Protein
63–67g Carbs
36–38g Fat

INGREDIENTS

Protein
4 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast

Sauce
1½ cups smoky barbecue sauce
6 oz tomato paste
2 tbsp Dijon mustard
2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
1 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp brown sugar
1 tbsp hot sauce
½ cup chicken broth

Seasoning Blend
2 tbsp smoked paprika
1½ tbsp chili powder
1 tbsp garlic powder
1 tbsp onion powder
2 tsp ground mustard
2 tsp black pepper
1½ tsp sea salt
1 tsp cayenne
½ tsp cinnamon

Finish
8 oz cream cheese
8 oz shredded sharp cheddar

Rice
3 cups dry jasmine rice
3 cups water

Cook HIGH 3 hours. Shred. Let reduce 10 minutes. Stir in cream cheese. Add cheddar and melt. Portion evenly into 10 servings.
