# High-Protein Lemon Chicken & Broccoli Meal Prep

## Ingredients

### The Base
- 3 lbs Chicken breast
- 24 oz Broccoli
- 1 cup White rice (washed)
- **Seasonings:** Salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and lemon pepper seasoning

### High-Protein Lemon Sauce
- 500g Plain Greek yogurt
- 50g Lemon juice
- 30g Minced garlic
- 50g Melted butter
- 30g Parmesan cheese

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Chicken
1. Place the chicken on a cutting board and tenderize it.
2. Coat the chicken thoroughly with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and lemon pepper seasoning.
3. Cook in a pan for **4 to 5 minutes per side** until you get a nice char/flavor.
4. Once cooked, chop or shred the chicken into bite-sized pieces.

### 2. Prepare the Sides
- **Broccoli:** Use a steamer with water and steam the broccoli for **10 to 15 minutes**. Chop it down slightly after steaming.
- **Rice:** Add water to your washed rice and cook until finished.

### 3. Make the Lemon Sauce
1. In a bowl, combine the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, minced garlic, melted butter, and Parmesan cheese.
2. Mix until smooth.

### 4. Combine and Serve
1. In a large container or bowl, combine the shredded chicken, steamed broccoli, and cooked rice.
2. Pour the high-protein lemon sauce over the mixture and mix thoroughly until well-coated.

> 💡 **Tip:** Don't forget to wash your dishes immediately so you don't get lazy with your meal prep!
