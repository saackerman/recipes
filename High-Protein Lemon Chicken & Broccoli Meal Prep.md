High-Protein Lemon Chicken & Broccoli Meal Prep
Ingredients
The Base:
3 lbs Chicken breast [00:04]
24 oz Broccoli [00:16]
1 cup White rice (washed) [00:30]
Seasonings: Salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and lemon pepper seasoning [00:09]
High-Protein Lemon Sauce:
500g Plain Greek yogurt [00:46]
50g Lemon juice [00:54]
30g Minced garlic [00:58]
50g Melted butter [01:03]
30g Parmesan cheese [01:08]

Instructions
1. Prepare the Chicken
Place the chicken on a cutting board and tenderize it [00:04].
Coat the chicken thoroughly with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and lemon pepper seasoning [00:09].
Cook in a pan for 4 to 5 minutes per side until you get a nice char/flavor [00:14].
Once cooked, chop or shred the chicken into bite-sized pieces [01:11].
2. Prepare the Sides
Broccoli: Use a steamer with water and steam the broccoli for 10 to 15 minutes [00:16]. Chop it down slightly after steaming [01:16].
Rice: Add water to your washed rice and cook until finished [00:34].
3. Make the Lemon Sauce
In a bowl, combine the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, minced garlic, melted butter, and Parmesan cheese [00:46].
Mix until smooth.
4. Combine and Serve
In a large container or bowl, combine the shredded chicken, steamed broccoli, and cooked rice [01:14].
Pour the high-protein lemon sauce over the mixture and mix thoroughly until well-coated [01:20].
Note: Don't forget to wash your dishes immediately so you don't get lazy with your meal prep! [01:42]
