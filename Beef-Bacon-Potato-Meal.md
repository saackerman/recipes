# High-Protein Beef, Bacon & Potato Meal Prep

A hearty, high-protein meal-prep recipe designed to support weight-loss goals and replace fast-food convenience. Makes multiple grab-and-go meals that reheat well.

## Yield

4–6 servings (depends on container size)

## Total time

About 1 hour (including roasting)

## Ingredients

### Protein
- 3 lb (1.36 kg) ground beef
- 10 slices beef bacon, chopped

### Vegetables
- 16 oz (450 g) onions, diced (about 2 medium)
- 3 bags Nibbles potatoes (or ~2–3 lb / 1–1.5 kg small potatoes), halved if large
- 16 oz (450 g) tomatoes, diced (about 2–3 medium)

### Dairy
- 1 block habanero cheese, grated (or ~8 oz / 225 g)

### Sauce & seasoning
- Bacon-flavored sea salt, to taste
- 1 cup (300 g) Blue Hog sugar-free BBQ sauce (or your favorite sugar-free BBQ sauce)

### Other
- Avocado oil spray (or a light coating of oil)

## Equipment
- Large skillet or sauté pan
- Baking sheet
- Grater
- Meal-prep containers

## Instructions

1. Cook the beef and onions
   - Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and diced onions. Season generously with bacon-flavored sea salt.
   - Break up the beef with a spatula and cook until browned and cooked through.

2. Add the beef bacon
   - Chop the beef bacon and add it to the pan with the cooked beef. Stir and cook a few minutes until the bacon is warmed and slightly crisp. (Tip: cover with a lid briefly to reduce splatter.)

3. Roast the potatoes
   - Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
   - Coat a baking sheet with avocado oil spray and spread the Nibbles potatoes in a single layer.
   - Roast for about 25–30 minutes, turning once halfway through, until golden and tender.

4. Grate the cheese
   - While the potatoes roast, grate the habanero cheese and set aside.

5. Combine everything
   - When the potatoes are done, add them to the skillet with the beef, bacon, and onions.
   - Stir in the diced tomatoes, grated cheese, and the sugar-free BBQ sauce. Mix thoroughly so the cheese melts and the sauce coats the mixture.

6. Portion and store
   - Divide the mixture evenly into meal-prep containers.
   - Refrigerate for up to 4 days or freeze for longer storage. Reheat in the microwave or in a skillet until warmed through.

## Notes & substitutions
- Adjust the amount of cheese or omit it to reduce calories.
- Substitute regular bacon or turkey bacon if beef bacon isn't available.
- Swap BBQ sauce for hot sauce, salsa, or a low-carb ketchup if preferred.
- If using larger potatoes, cut into 1" pieces so they roast evenly.
