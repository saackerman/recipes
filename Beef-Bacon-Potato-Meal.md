High-Protein Beef, Bacon & Potato Meal Prep
This high-protein meal prep is designed to help with weight loss and provide a convenient alternative to fast food.

Ingredients
Protein: 3 lbs Ground Beef [00:00], 10 slices Beef Bacon [00:10]

Vegetables: 16 oz Onions (diced) [00:03], 3 bags Nibbles Potatoes [00:21], 16 oz Tomatoes (diced) [00:35]

Dairy: 1 block Habanero Cheese (grated) [00:30]

Sauce & Seasoning: Bacon-flavored sea salt [00:06], Blue Hog Sugar-Free BBQ Sauce (300g or 1 cup) [00:36]

Other: Avocado oil spray [00:20]

Instructions
1. Cook the Beef & Onions [00:00]
Add 3 lbs of ground beef and 16 oz of diced onions to a pan.

Season heavily with bacon-flavored sea salt [00:06].

Break up the meat and let it cook until browned.

2. Prepare the Beef Bacon [00:10]
Chop 10 slices of beef bacon and add them to the pan with the beef.

Cover with a lid to prevent grease from splattering [00:16].

3. Roast the Potatoes [00:20]
Coat a baking sheet with a decent layer of avocado oil spray.

Add 3 bags of Nibbles potatoes to the sheet.

Bake at 425°F for 30 minutes [00:28].

4. Grate the Cheese [00:30]
While the potatoes roast, grate an entire block of habanero cheese.

5. Combine Everything [00:32]
Once the beef is done and the potatoes are roasted, combine them in the large pan.

Add the grated cheese, 16 oz of diced tomatoes, and 1 cup of sugar-free BBQ sauce [00:35].

Mix everything thoroughly.

6. Portion Out [00:40]
Divide the mixture into meal prep containers for easy grab-and-go meals.
