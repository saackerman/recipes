# Turkey Burger Bowls

**Makes:** 6 servings (1 bowl per serving)

## Macros (per serving)
- **Calories:** 465
- **Fat:** 17 g
- **Carbs:** 39 g
- **Protein:** 39 g

## Ingredients
- 2 lb ground turkey (97/3) (907 g)
- 6 medium russet potatoes (~200 g each)
- 1 onion
- 2 tomatoes
- 1/2 cup pickle chips (~40 g)
- 4 cups lettuce
- 8 slices American cheese
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp pepper

## Burger sauce
- 2 tbsp ketchup (34 g)
- 2 tbsp 0% Greek yogurt (30 g)
- 2 tbsp light mayo (30 g)
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard (30 g)
- 2 tsp pickle juice (10 g)

## Instructions
1. Air fry diced potatoes at **400°F** for **18–20 minutes**, until crispy.
2. Cook onion and ground turkey with seasonings; top with cheese.
3. Mix burger sauce ingredients together.
4. Divide potatoes into containers; top with turkey, veggies, and sauce.
5. Enjoy now, or store up to **1 week**.
