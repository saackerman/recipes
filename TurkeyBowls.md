TURKEY BURGER BOWLS (Makes 6 Servings: Each Serving is 1 Bowl)

-17g Fat
-39g Carbs
-39g Protein

Ingredients:
-2 lbs Ground Turkey (97/3) (907g)
-6 Medium Russet Potatoes (~200g each)
-1 Onion
-2 Tomatoes
-1/2 Cup Pickle Chips (~40g)
-4 Cups of Lettuce
-8 Slices of American Cheese
-1 Tbsp Garlic Powder
-1 Tbsp Paprika
-1 Tbsp Salt
-1 Tbsp Pepper

Burger Sauce:
-2 Tbsp Ketchup (34g)
-2 Tbsp 0% Greek Yogurt (30g)
-2 Tbsp Light Mayo (30g)
-2 Tbsp Yellow Mustard (30g)
-2 Tsp Pickle Juice (10g)

Instructions:
1.) Air fry diced potatoes at 400°F for 18–20 min until crispy.
2.) Cook onion and ground turkey with seasonings; top with cheese.
3.) Mix burger sauce.
4.) Divide potatoes into containers, top with turkey, veggies, and sauce.
5.) Enjoy now or store up to a week.
