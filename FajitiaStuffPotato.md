# Fajita Stuffed Potatoes

## Macros (per serving)
- **Calories:** 570
- **Fat:** 14 g
- **Carbs:** 52 g
- **Protein:** 59 g

## Ingredients
- 2 lb chicken breast (907 g)
- 1 onion
- 3 bell peppers
- 1 (10 oz) can diced tomatoes with chilies (283 g)
- 5 medium russet potatoes (~200 g each)
- 2 cups reduced-fat Mexican-style cheese (226 g)
- 2 packets fajita seasoning

## Instructions
1. Cook chicken with onion, peppers, fajita seasoning, and tomatoes in a crockpot (**high** 3–4 hours or **low** 6–8 hours).
2. Bake fork-pierced potatoes at **350°F** for ~60 minutes, until tender.
3. Shred the chicken and split the potatoes.
4. Top potatoes with chicken and cheese; bake 2–3 minutes until the cheese melts.
5. Cool slightly and enjoy.
