# 🥣 Buffalo Ranch High-Protein Mac Bowls

**Source:** [Cookingforgains (YouTube Shorts)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/zNTURkMY7JU)
**Yields:** 10 Servings
**Focus:** High Protein / Meal Prep

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### The Protein & Pasta

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken Breast | 3 lbs (trimmed and tenderized) |
| Pasta | 16 oz (regular or protein pasta) |
| Bacon Bits | 1 bag of real bacon bits |

### The Sauce & Cheese

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fat-Free Cheddar | 1 bag (shredded) |
| Fat-Free Mozzarella | 1 bag (shredded) |
| American Cheese | 3 slices |
| Buffalo Sauce | 1 cup Frank's RedHot |
| Fat-Free Ranch Dressing | 1 cup |
| Ranch Seasoning | 1 packet |
| Sun-dried Tomatoes | 1 bag |

### Chicken Seasonings

- Sea salt flakes
- Black pepper
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder
- Low-calorie oil spray

---

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

1. **Prep the Chicken:** Trim excess fat and use a meat tenderizer to flatten the meat so it cooks evenly.
2. **Season & Sear:** Season with salt, pepper, garlic, and onion powder. Cook in a skillet over medium-high heat with a heavy layer of oil spray.
3. **Boil Pasta:** While the chicken is cooking, boil 16 oz of pasta.
4. **Chop & Combine:** Chop the cooked chicken into pieces. In a large pot, add the chicken, sun-dried tomatoes, and bacon bits.
5. **The Cheesy Mix:** Add the shredded cheddar, mozzarella, and the 3 slices of American cheese.
6. **Sauce it Up:** Add the ranch seasoning packet, fat-free ranch, and Frank's RedHot. Add the cooked pasta and mix until fully combined.
7. **Portion:** Divide the mixture into 10 separate meal prep containers.

---

## ⏲️ Reheating Instructions

- **From Fridge:** Microwave for 60 seconds.
- **From Freezer:** Add a small splash of water and cover with a napkin. Microwave for 2 minutes and 30 seconds.
