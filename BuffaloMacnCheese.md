Ingredients
The Protein & Pasta:

Chicken Breast: 3 lbs (trimmed and tenderized) [00:08]

Pasta: 16 oz (regular or protein pasta) [00:39]

Bacon Bits: 1 bag of real bacon bits [00:54]

The Sauce & Cheese:

Fat-Free Cheddar: 1 bag [00:56]

Fat-Free Mozzarella: 1 bag [00:58]

American Cheese: 3 slices [01:06]

Buffalo Sauce: 1 cup Frank’s RedHot [01:04]

Fat-Free Ranch: 1 cup [01:02]

Ranch Seasoning: 1 packet [01:01]

Sun-dried Tomatoes: 1 bag [00:52]

Seasonings for Chicken:

Sea salt flakes, black pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder [00:19]

Low-calorie oil spray [00:31]

Instructions
Prepare the Chicken: Trim the fat off the chicken breasts and use a meat tenderizer to flatten them for even cooking [00:09].

Season & Cook: Place the chicken in a bowl and season with salt, pepper, garlic, and onion powder. Cook in a skillet over medium-high heat using a heavy coating of oil spray [00:27].

Boil Pasta: While the chicken finishes, boil 16 oz of your preferred pasta until cooked [00:39].

Chop & Combine: Once cooked, chop the chicken into bite-sized pieces [00:46]. In a large pot, combine the chopped chicken, cooked pasta, sun-dried tomatoes, bacon bits, both bags of shredded cheese, and the American cheese slices [00:49].

Sauce it Up: Add the ranch seasoning packet, 1 cup of fat-free ranch, and 1 cup of buffalo sauce [01:01]. Mix everything thoroughly until the cheese is melted and the pasta is well-coated [01:08].

Portioning: Divide the mixture into 10 meal prep containers [01:14].

Reheating Tips
From Fridge: Microwave for 60 seconds [01:16].

From Freezer: Add a small splash of water and cover with a napkin. Microwave for 2 minutes and 30 seconds [01:19].
