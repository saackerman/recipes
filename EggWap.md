This high-protein, low-calorie Egg Caesar Wrap under 400 calories and 49g of protein. It takes about 20 minutes from start to finish.

Instructions
1. Make the Egg Wrap Base
Whisk Egg Whites: Using a mixer, whisk egg whites on high for about 4 minutes until they form stiff peaks [00:00:20].

Season: Gently stir in garlic powder, parsley, and salt [00:00:26].

Prep Pan: Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and give it a quick spray of oil [00:00:40].

Spread: Dump the egg white mixture onto the pan and spread it out as wide as the pan [00:00:40].

Bake: Cook in the oven at 400°F for 10 minutes [00:00:51].

2. Prepare the Caesar Sauce
While the wrap is baking, mix the following ingredients [00:00:57]:

Non-fat Greek yogurt

Garlic powder & onion powder

Chopped parsley

Freshly grated Parmesan cheese

A squeeze of lemon

Salt & pepper

Note: The creator also recommends adding Dijon mustard (which she forgot in the video) [00:01:56].

3. Assembly
Remove the wrap from the oven and spread the Caesar sauce over it [00:01:24].

Add two fried eggs (with runny yolks) on top and season with salt [00:01:30].

Add more sauce if desired, then fold the wrap over [00:01:38].
