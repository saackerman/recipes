# Egg Caesar Wrap (High-Protein, Low-Calorie)

This high-protein, low-calorie Egg Caesar Wrap is under 400 calories and has **49g of protein**. It takes about **20 minutes** from start to finish.

## Video

Watch the full video here: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k

## Instructions

### 1) Make the Egg Wrap Base

1. **Whisk egg whites:** Using a mixer, whisk egg whites on high for about 4 minutes until they form stiff peaks. *([00:00:20](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=20))*
2. **Season:** Gently stir in garlic powder, parsley, and salt. *([00:00:26](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=26))*
3. **Prep pan:** Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and give it a quick spray of oil. *([00:00:40](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=40))*
4. **Spread:** Dump the egg white mixture onto the pan and spread it out as wide as the pan. *([00:00:40](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=40))*
5. **Bake:** Cook in the oven at **400°F** for **10 minutes**. *([00:00:51](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=51))*

### 2) Prepare the Caesar Sauce

While the wrap is baking, mix the following ingredients. *([00:00:57](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=57))*

- Non-fat Greek yogurt
- Garlic powder & onion powder
- Chopped parsley
- Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- A squeeze of lemon
- Salt & pepper

> **Note:** The creator also recommends adding Dijon mustard (which she forgot in the video). *([00:01:56](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=116))*

### 3) Assembly

1. Remove the wrap from the oven and spread the Caesar sauce over it. *([00:01:24](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=84))*
2. Add two fried eggs (with runny yolks) on top and season with salt. *([00:01:30](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=90))*
3. Add more sauce if desired, then fold the wrap over. *([00:01:38](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/n2LasJxYf0k?t=98))*
