Recipe Overview: Sweet Potato Sliders 🍔
This recipe uses thick-cut sweet potato discs as a healthy, nutrient-dense replacement for traditional burger buns.

Instructions:

Prep [00:06]: Peel and chop sweet potatoes into equal discs (approx. 1/2" thick).

Bake [00:10]: Arrange in a dish, brush with coconut oil, and season with salt. Bake at 450°F for 20 minutes [00:15].

Beef [00:17]: Cook your slider-sized burger patties in a pan.

Sear [00:19]: After baking, sear the potato discs in a skillet with butter for extra crispiness.

Assemble [00:23]: Place a patty on a potato disc and top with raw cheddar cheese [00:25].

Toppings [00:28]: Add mashed avocado [00:29] and pickles [00:30].

Finalize [00:32]: Top with another potato disc and serve.
