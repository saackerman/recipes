# Sweet Potato Sliders

This recipe uses thick-cut sweet potato discs as a healthy, nutrient-dense replacement for traditional burger buns.

## Instructions (with video timestamps)

- [Prep (0:06)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=6): Peel and chop sweet potatoes into equal discs (approx. 1/2" thick).
- [Bake (0:10)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=10): Arrange in a dish, brush with coconut oil, and season with salt.
  - Bake at 450°F for 20 minutes. [See 0:15](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=15)
- [Beef (0:17)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=17): Cook your slider-sized burger patties in a pan.
- [Sear (0:19)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=19): After baking, sear the potato discs in a skillet with butter for extra crispiness.
- [Assemble (0:23)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=23): Place a patty on a potato disc and top with raw cheddar cheese.
  - [Cheese (0:25)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=25)
- [Toppings (0:28)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=28): Add mashed avocado and pickles.
  - [Avocado (0:29)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=29)
  - [Pickles (0:30)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=30)
- [Finalize (0:32)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UfdRErUGreM?t=32): Top with another potato disc and serve.
