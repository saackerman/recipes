# High Protein Reese's Snack Balls

*Makes approximately 25 balls*

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Protein Powder (Reese's flavor recommended) | 120g |
| Oat Flour | 60g |
| Almond Milk | 65g |
| Sugar-Free Syrup | 50g |
| Peanut Butter | 60g |
| Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chips *(for topping)* | 60g |
| Sea Salt | For garnish |

---

## Instructions

1. **Mix the Base:** In a large bowl, combine the protein powder, oat flour, almond milk, sugar-free syrup, and peanut butter. [&#x23F1;](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YmYYrEsNLMg&t=15)

2. **Form the Dough:** Stir until the mixture becomes thick. Once it reaches a dough-like consistency, use your hands to press and fold the mixture until it forms a solid dough ball. [&#x23F1;](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YmYYrEsNLMg&t=21)

3. **Roll the Balls:** Rip off small pieces of the dough and roll them between your palms to create small spheres. This recipe should yield approximately 25 balls. [&#x23F1;](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YmYYrEsNLMg&t=34)

4. **Melt the Chocolate:** Place the chocolate chips in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring in between, until completely smooth. [&#x23F1;](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YmYYrEsNLMg&t=44)

5. **Coat and Season:** Drizzle the melted chocolate over the balls using a circular motion. Finish by sprinkling sea salt over the top. [&#x23F1;](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YmYYrEsNLMg&t=61)

6. **Chill:** Place the balls in the fridge for 15 minutes to set before serving. [&#x23F1;](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YmYYrEsNLMg&t=69)
