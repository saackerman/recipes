5 High Protein Reese's Snack Balls.

Ingredients
Protein Powder: 120g (Reese's flavor recommended) [00:04]

Oat Flour: 60g [00:07]

Almond Milk: 65g [00:08]

Sugar-Free Syrup: 50g [00:10]

Peanut Butter: 60g [00:13]

Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chips: 60g (for topping) [00:41]

Sea Salt: For garnish [01:06]

Instructions
Mix the Base: In a large bowl, combine the protein powder, oat flour, almond milk, sugar-free syrup, and peanut butter. [00:15]

Form the Dough: Stir until the mixture becomes thick. Once it reaches a dough-like consistency, use your hands to press and fold the mixture until it forms a solid dough ball. [00:21]

Roll the Balls: Rip off small pieces of the dough and roll them between your palms to create small spheres. This recipe should yield approximately 25 balls. [00:34]

Melt the Chocolate: Place the chocolate chips in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring in between, until completely smooth. [00:44]

Coat and Season: Drizzle the melted chocolate over the balls using a circular motion. Finish by sprinkling sea salt over the top. [01:01]

Chill: Place the balls in the fridge for 15 minutes to set before serving. [01:09]
